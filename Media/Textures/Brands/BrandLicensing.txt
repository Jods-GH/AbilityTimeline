GitHub logo belongs to GitHub: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo (https://github.com/logos redirects there)
GitHub_logo.tga and GitHub_logo_black.tga are the white and black Invertocat (GitHub_Invertocat_White.png,
GitHub_Invertocat_Black.png) of the official package https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip, converted to TGA
pixel for pixel: same size (294x288), colors and shape.

Legal info:

GITHUB, the GITHUB logo design, the INVERTOCAT logo design, OCTOCAT and the OCTOCAT logo design are trademarks of
GitHub, Inc., registered in the United States and other countries.

How it is used here, as the brand page allows: the Invertocat only marks a button that links to the addon's
GitHub project (its issues), less prominent than the addon's own name and logo.

Following the brand page, it must not:
Imply that GitHub made, endorses or is affiliated with the addon.
Be used as the addon's own logo or in its name.
Be modified: no other colors, proportions, shadows, gradients, or combining it with other words or designs.
Be shown on busy or low-contrast backgrounds: the white mark is shown on dark buttons, the black one on light ones.
